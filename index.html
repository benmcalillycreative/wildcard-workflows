Questions

Before you open it

What's n8n?

A tool that runs automations for you, like a little routine that follows a recipe. Use n8n Cloud, or run it yourself for free.

How hard is setup?

If you've used n8n before, about fifteen minutes a box. New to it? Plan on an hour for the first one: you make an n8n account, sign in to Google once and copy a starter sheet. Each box after that goes faster. The guide covers every step, and the help page covers the usual snags.

Why a surprise?

Because opening it is the fun part. Everyone who buys a given box gets the same three tricks, so it's a surprise, not a gamble.

Can I peek first?

You can see the theme and every app a box uses. The tricks stay in the carton until you open it.

Is this a subscription?

No. Pay once and keep the files.
