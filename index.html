You need an n8n account, either n8n Cloud or self-hosted. The setup guide walks you through importing each workflow and connecting your apps.

Choosing what to automate is the hard part. We pick the workflows worth having for your niche, and the value floor means you never get filler.

Not the workflows themselves. That's the seal. You can see the niche and every app a box needs before you buy.

What if a workflow doesn't work for me?

Is this a subscription?

No. One payment, and the files are yours to keep. New boxes drop regularly.
