No. 1

Getting started

What do I need before I start?

An n8n account (n8n Cloud, or n8n running on your own computer or server), a Google account for the Gmail and Sheets steps, and the box's Setup Guide.pdf. That's it. Every box lists its apps on the carton.

If you've used n8n before, a box takes about fifteen minutes. New to it? Plan on an hour for the first one. The ones after that go faster, since your accounts are already connected.

How do I import a workflow?
  1. Unzip the box.
  2. In n8n, create a new, empty workflow.
  3. Open the ⋯ menu at the top right and choose Import from File, then pick one of the .json files.

Do one workflow at a time. Each has a Start here note on the canvas.

Where do I get the starter sheet?

Use the link in your guide and click Make a copy. The copy is yours, in your own Google Drive. Here are the links:

Family Fun sheet · Friend Group sheet · Mischief sheet

Daily Delight doesn't use a sheet. If the link won't open, the .xlsx in your box is the same sheet: upload it to Google Drive, open it with Google Sheets, then choose File › Save as Google Sheets.

I tested it, and it worked. Now what?

Switch it on so it runs by itself. In newer n8n versions that's the Publish button at the top of the workflow. In older ones it's the Active toggle. A workflow that isn't switched on only runs when you click Execute workflow.

No. 2

Connecting accounts

A step has a red warning triangle.

That step needs to know which account to use. Open it, click the Credential box, and choose Create new credential. On n8n Cloud, click Sign in with Google and approve. After the first time, just pick that same credential from the list in every other Google step.

It worked yesterday, and today it says my credentials failed.

Google sign-ins sometimes expire, or get cut off if you change your Google password. Open the credential (under Overview › Credentials), click Reconnect or sign in again, and save.

I run n8n myself, and there's no "Sign in with Google" button.

A self-hosted n8n needs its own Google sign-in setup: a one-time OAuth app in Google Cloud. Search for "n8n Google OAuth2 credentials" to find n8n's walkthrough. It takes about fifteen minutes. On n8n Cloud, you can skip this.

Google says the app "hasn't been verified."

On a self-hosted n8n, that's your own OAuth app, so Google is warning you about yourself. Click Advanced, then continue. On n8n Cloud you shouldn't see this. If you do, close the window and try the sign-in again.

No. 3

Sheets

I pasted my sheet's link, and the Sheet box went back to "From list."

That's normal. n8n clears the tab whenever the document changes. Click the Sheet box and choose the tab that step's name points to (Get Family wants the Family tab). Or switch it to By Name and type the tab name. Then click Execute step to check it reads your rows.

Which link do I paste?

The one in your browser's address bar while your copy of the sheet is open. It starts with https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/. Paste the same link into every Sheets step in that box.

A Sheets step can't find a column, or the data comes out blank.

The workflows find columns by name, so row 1 of each tab has to match the starter sheet exactly, with the same spelling and capitals. Renamed a tab or a header? Change it back. Then open the step again so n8n reloads the columns.

It's using my example rows.

Grey italic rows that start with e.g. are examples. Replace them with your own, or delete them.

No. 4

Timing

It ran, but nothing was sent.

Several tricks stay quiet on purpose when there's nothing to say: no birthday coming up, no sky event tonight, a cloudy sunset. Open the Executions tab at the top of the workflow and click the latest run. If the step just before the send step shows no items, that's why.

It sends at the wrong hour.

n8n is probably using a different time zone. Open the workflow's ⋯ › Settings and set Timezone to yours.

The weather or sunset is for the wrong place.

Daily Delight uses the latitude and longitude in Your Details. In Google Maps, right-click your town and click the numbers at the top of the menu to copy them. The first number is latitude, the second is longitude.

Sunset Alarm's test never finishes.

It's waiting for the alarm time on purpose: it sends twenty minutes before sunset. Test it in the afternoon, or switch it on and let tonight be the test.

Secret Mission skipped the Friday reveal.

n8n only remembers the week's agent during real, scheduled runs, not manual tests. The first reveal comes the Friday after the first real Monday.

No. 5

Discord & Slack

Nothing shows up in the channel.

Check two things in Your Details. chatWebhookUrl must be the whole webhook link. chatApp must say discord or slack, matching where the link came from. If someone deleted the webhook, make a new one and paste that in.

The message posts, but the bold text looks odd.

chatApp is set to the other app. Discord and Slack format bold text differently, and the workflow switches formats based on that setting.

Can it read our chat?

No. An incoming webhook can only post into one channel. Nothing in the Mischief box reads messages.

No. 6

Your order

I paid, but no email came.

Your box arrives within a few minutes, sent to the address you used at checkout. Check spam, promotions and updates. Still nothing after half an hour? Reply to your Stripe receipt and we'll send it by hand.

The people on my list get my emails in spam.

These emails come from your own Gmail. Ask them to mark one as Not spam, or to add your address to their contacts. That usually fixes it for good.

Something won't import or run as described.

Reply to your Stripe receipt. Say which box and which workflow, and what you see, and a screenshot helps a lot. We'll fix it or refund the box.
